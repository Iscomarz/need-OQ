## Platform

web

## Stack

SvelteKit 2 + Tailwind CSS v4 + TypeScript + Svelte 5 (Runes)

## Users

Oil and gas pipeline operators, industrial contractors, safety directors, and field technicians needing PHMSA 49 CFR 192/195 Operator Qualification (OQ) compliance across the Permian Basin, Eagle Ford, Gulf Coast, and nationwide.

## Product Purpose

Deliver nationwide Operator Qualification (OQ) pipeline evaluations, NCCER Master Evaluator assessments, multi-platform verification (Veriforce, EWN, MEA, ITS, OQSG), and specialized safety orientations. Drives structured class registrations and immediate phone inquiries.

## Positioning

"Need OQ by Safe Hands Safety" — Born and bred in the oil patch. Either oil or gas, we speak your language! Performing nationwide OQs since 1999 via Mobile Field Labs, fixed ATAC Centers (Midland & Corpus Christi), and Remote Proctoring (CBT).

## Operating Context

Busy safety managers and contractors under strict PHMSA compliance deadlines, needing rapid field turnaround, zero audit findings, and streamlined class registration.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Dedicated multi-page SvelteKit structure:
  - `/` (Home overview & navigation hub)
  - `/oq-pipeline` (Platforms: Veriforce, EWN, NCCER, MEA, ITS, OQSG)

  - `/accreditations` (Basin United, SafeLand, Gold Shovel, H2S Clear, etc.)
  - `/locations` (Midland ATAC Center, Corpus Christi, Mobile nationwide units)
  - `/faq` (PHMSA OQ rules & evaluation procedures)
  - `/quote` (Complete structured class registration & quote form)
- Form routing: Transmitted directly via Resend to `Joyce.Sanchez@SafeHandsSafety.com`.
